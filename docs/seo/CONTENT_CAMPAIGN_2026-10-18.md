# Publication campaign: practical multilingual resources

## Selection

Use the six-language research and authorized Search Console baseline already
captured on 18 October. Final GSC data ends on 15 October and is not evidence
of post-release ranking. There are no measured Keyword Planner volumes.

This batch prioritizes two distinct needs: a classroom workshop that can be
facilitated, and a case-based explanation of recruitment AI. Existing IT/EN/FR
pages already own those topics. Complete their German, Spanish and European
Portuguese equivalents instead of adding another set of near-duplicate pages.
Strengthen all six existing education hubs with contextual reading paths.

One owner per intent/language. Workshop resources contain original instructions,
a four-field worksheet, a suggested 60-minute sequence, a fictional case,
discussion criteria and adaptations. Recruitment resources contain a fictional
case, evidence requests, human-review questions, limits and official sources.
The new main sections contain about 500-580 words per page, excluding navigation,
repeated links and the footer; length is not represented as a ranking factor.

## Evidence and accuracy

- Research sources and exploratory search strings are in the six language briefs.
  They are not geographically controlled Google SERP or demand measurements.
- Classification assertions checked against Article 6 and Annex III, point 4,
  in the Commission's AI Act Service Desk and the EUR-Lex consolidated text
  dated 27 July 2026. No new deadlines or blanket claims about all HR software.
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-6
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/de/ai-act/annex-3
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/es/ai-act/annex-3
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PT/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727
- Fictional classroom cases and suggested timings are project-authored proposals,
  not measured classroom outcomes, real employers or particular game dossiers.
- Existing project screenshots are explicitly captioned as archived version 3,
  not passed off as current version 4 or translated gameplay.
- No professional native-speaker or legal certification is claimed. AI assistance
  remains disclosed. No accredited course, institutional endorsement or learning
  efficacy is invented.

## Release and measurement boundaries

Publish through a reviewed branch/PR and existing build/browser gates. Verify
all 152 public routes, new text, source links, images, canonical/hreflang and
mobile layouts. Submit the existing six sitemap URLs only after the new live
XML is verified. Sitemap receipt is not indexing or ranking.

No changes to DNS, SSL, apex redirect Worker, beta, dashboard, gameplay or data
collection. Do not imply that more candidate keywords means more rankings.
Evaluate crawl/index discovery and then complete comparable post-release periods,
with separate brand/non-brand and landing-page-language views. No automatic
monitor, email outreach, paid links or ongoing scheduled work is created here.
