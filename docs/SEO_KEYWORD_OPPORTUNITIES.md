## Evidence and priority

1. Game discovery: first priority is category + brand. The updated home headings
   implement this focus in all six languages. Do not pursue the ambiguous phrase
   "no ai" or misrepresent the project as an anti-regulation campaign.
2. Teachers: distinguish classroom facilitation from the regulation's treatment
   of school AI systems. Updated FR/DE/ES/PT introductions implement the latter
   focus; IT/EN/FR classroom-session pages retain their own separate intent.
3. Literacy and introductory guides: use the existing pages above. These are
   further editorial opportunities, not a claim that every candidate was added
   verbatim in this release. Review actual reader questions and new query/page
   evidence before expanding content. Short new-language pages need specialist
   and native review, not copied long-form promises or artificial padding.
