# NO AI ACT 4.0.0

18 October 2026. Public-release preparation authorized by the project owner.
Publication is confirmed only by the successful deployment and live checks.

## Changes

- IT/EN/FR game and 107 public site routes.
- Contextual tutorial, clearer evaluation explanations, debrief and review.
- Turno, Libera, Sorpresa and Ripasso with local save compatibility preserved.
- HTML mobile presentation and keyboard/accessibility improvements.
- Existing press carousel and source attribution preserved.
- Automatic first-party aggregate usage counters, with visible notice and
  disable control, no consent checkbox. DNT and GPC always override collection.
- No player accounts, names, email addresses, answers, outcomes or scores sent.
- Only delivery receipts (short-lived hashed random delivery codes) and daily
  marginal counters. Existing private Access dashboard remains protected.

## Scope and Limits

Counters are events, not unique players or educational-effectiveness evidence.
Daily report cells below 10 events are suppressed. A missing completion is not
proof of abandonment. No retrospective reconstruction of earlier play is possible.

Disabling collection clears pending data for the current page; reloading enables
it again as disclosed. Browser privacy signals cannot be overridden. No analytics
cookies or stored opt-out preferences are added.

The existing stable local save key/schema is retained; beta saves stay isolated.
Press screenshots remain explicitly archival version-3 material, not new captures.
No new cases, legal conclusions or educational-validation claims are introduced.

Human playtesting, NVDA/VoiceOver and native-speaker review remain distinct from
automated verification. No legal certification of the analytics system is claimed.
